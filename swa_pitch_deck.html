The Experience

Here is how it actually fits into a user's day.

You wake up, do a 30-second check-in. During the day, you receive small nudges to notice your state. In the evening, you reflect on friction points.

Because these take less than a minute, it becomes effortless. And over weeks and months, these tiny interactions accumulate into deep, personalized insights.

The Differentiator

This brings us to our biggest differentiator: Signal vs Label.

Most apps try to diagnose you immediately. SWA knows that a single moment—like hesitating in class—does not mean you are an insecure person. It's just a signal.

SWA's intelligence lies in collecting hundreds of these lightweight signals over time. When SWA sees a cluster, it identifies the underlying pattern—like a fear of evaluation—and brings it to your awareness.

A reflective intelligence layer.

AI helps you notice yourself better. It doesn't decide who you are.
