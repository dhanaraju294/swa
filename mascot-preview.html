Same character, a changing rhythm. Sprout, steady and blooming share the same silhouette and happy face; their greens change. Quiet pause uses a muted palette and a sad face. The app gently floats the whole illustration ±3 px over 5.2 seconds; this preview mirrors that motion and respects reduced-motion preferences.
State selection uses the effective streak. Home also treats a completed flow with its next day locked as activity today.
